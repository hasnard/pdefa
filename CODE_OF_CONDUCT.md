# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation
in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our standards

Positive behavior:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness
- Being respectful of differing opinions
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Accepting responsibility and apologizing
- Focusing on what is best for the community

Unacceptable behavior:

- Sexualized language or imagery
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, personal attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information
- Other unprofessional conduct

## Enforcement

Report to **info@pdfduty.com**. All complaints will be reviewed and
investigated promptly and fairly.
